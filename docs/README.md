# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is the entry point to OctoAcme's project management guidance. OctoAcme manages work through a lifecycle that begins with validating a business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, and readiness to proceed. Planning then turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified risks and dependencies. During execution, teams deliver incremental work, track progress on a project board, and adapt as they learn.

Project delivery is shared across clear roles. Project Managers coordinate plans, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize work, and measure impact; Developers implement, test, and review changes; and QA/testing validates quality and acceptance. Stakeholders contribute context and approvals. Regular standups, weekly delivery and PM/Product syncs, milestone demos, and stakeholder updates keep progress visible. Risks and blockers are tracked and escalated from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the Sponsor when needed.

Quality is integrated throughout delivery through clear acceptance criteria, small pull requests, code review, automated CI tests and linting, and appropriate unit, integration, smoke, security, and manual acceptance testing. Releases require passing checks, release notes, smoke tests, deployment verification, and a rollback or mitigation plan. After sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents, retrospectives capture lessons and assign a small number of actionable improvements with owners and follow-up.
